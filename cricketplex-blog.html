CricketPlex Guide

Welcome to CricketPlex

CricketPlex is built for managers who want more than just a match result. Here, you build your club, manage your squad, plan across formats, develop players over time, compete in leagues, take on friendly rivals, and shape the future of your team one decision at a time.

If you are new to the game, this guide will walk you through what the site offers and how each area of CricketPlex fits into your journey as a manager.

Start Your Journey

Your story in CricketPlex begins with signing up, creating your club, and choosing the identity you want to carry into every league and every season.

Once your team is set up, you are not entering a temporary session. You are entering a club world where your players, finances, fixtures, fans, and league history continue to matter as you move forward.

What you do first: create your account, set up your team, choose your country, and step into the manager dashboard.

Your Club Dashboard

The dashboard is your home base. It is designed to give you a quick read on your club at any moment. From here, you can see your team identity, upcoming matches, league progress, weather, morale, fan growth, home ground context, trophies, and recent club activity.

Instead of making you open five different pages to understand the current state of your team, CricketPlex pulls the essentials together in one place so you can decide what matters next.

  • Check your latest match state and upcoming fixtures.
  • Track your league position across formats.
  • Watch morale, fans, and club momentum.
  • Stay aware of weather and recent events around your team.

Compete Across Three Formats

CricketPlex is built around three different competitive identities: T20, ODI, and First-Class cricket. That means your club is not limited to one style of play. You manage the same club across formats that demand different thinking.

T20

Quick, high-impact cricket where momentum swings fast and every decision feels immediate.

ODI

A middle ground where balance matters and squad structure starts to show its value over longer innings.

FC

The deeper format, where strategy stretches across days and long-term planning matters most.

One Club, Many Challenges

Your squad, training choices, and team development all feed into how your club performs across these formats.

Follow Your Matches From Start to Finish

Matches in CricketPlex are not hidden behind a single simulate button. Each fixture has its own flow, starting before the toss and continuing through strategy, live play, commentary, and final scorecards.

Before the match: review the fixture preview, check the pitch, prepare your lineup, and get ready.
During the match: move into the live match center or commentary experience and follow the action.
After the match: review the result, scorecard, and the impact on your club.

This gives every fixture more presence. A match feels like an event, not just a background calculation.

First-Class Cricket Has Its Own Rhythm

First-Class matches in CricketPlex are designed to feel different. They are not treated like longer limited-overs matches. They unfold with their own pacing, their own decisions, and their own between-day tension.

If your club is playing FC cricket, you can experience multi-day match flow, day-based progression, and strategy choices that matter beyond a single innings.

  • Pause after day 1 and prepare for day 2.
  • Adjust FC strategy when the match situation changes.
  • Make decisions around declarations and longer-form planning.
FC is where patient managers can really feel the depth of CricketPlex.

Grow Players Through the Academy

The academy is where you invest in the future of your club. It gives you a steady connection to youth development and lets you shape player growth through focused training.

You can pull youth players, manage training assignments, review training history, and improve academy capacity over time. This gives long-term squad planning a real place inside the game.

  • Pull new youth players on a weekly cycle.
  • Choose focused training types for selected players.
  • Track how players improve over time.
  • Upgrade the academy to unlock more focused development slots.

Use the Transfer Market to Reshape Your Squad

Not every squad problem is solved through training. Sometimes you need the market. CricketPlex includes a transfer market where managers can browse player listings, place bids, track ongoing auctions, and manage their own listings.

This makes squad building more dynamic. A player is not just an item in a menu. He can be contested, valued differently by different clubs, and won or lost through bidding.

  • Browse active transfer listings.
  • Filter by role, age, ratings, skills, and player profile details.
  • Place bids in live auctions.
  • Track your own listings and completed transfer activity.

Challenge Other Managers

League cricket gives structure. Friendly challenges give freedom. In CricketPlex, you can send direct challenges to other managers, choose the format, select a pitch type, set a match date and time, and create your own side contests outside league play.

This is a great way to test your team, enjoy rivalry matches, or simply play more often against other human-managed clubs.
